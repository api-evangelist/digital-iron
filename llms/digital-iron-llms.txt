# Digital Iron

> Digital Iron is an AI platform delivering aftersales intelligence for heavy machinery. It helps equipment dealers and OEMs turn scattered service data — machine history, product documentation, customer records, bills of materials, work orders, and technician notes — into faster troubleshooting, accurate parts identification, and reliable job quoting.

Digital Iron serves the heavy machinery aftersales market (excavators, crushers, compaction rollers, and similar equipment). By aggregating and reasoning over service data, the platform surfaces recurring failure patterns across fleets to improve first-time fix rates. It is backed by Seedcamp and tracked in the API Evangelist network as a portfolio company. No public API, developer portal, or SDKs have been identified at the time of writing; the product is delivered as a hosted application.
